What I'm taking away

The biggest lesson from building this: prompts are how my agents think, code is how my shop stays safe. Every time something went wrong in a way that could cost money or embarrass the shop, the fix that actually stuck was code:

  • paying only through a human click;
  • amounts pulled from the database, never from the agent;
  • no double payments;
  • no pricing below cost;
  • no misnamed products;
  • the backend recording Boss's call.

The judgment calls are where the agents earned their keep, and also where they wandered. Boss sending 102 to Facilities and nobody else was perfect judgment. Writing Tauhid a reply that promised no date before I'd signed #501 was perfect judgment. But 103's status flipping between runs, and Accounting drafting a $264 PO it told me not to approve, is judgment wandering. My team handled tickets 101, 102 and 103 and got the money exactly right, $3,400 down to $160, both payments signed by me. That's the part I'd bet the shop on. What it can't do yet is take in the $417.60 from the Yale AI Club or send Tauhid his email, and with $2,400 rent due 10/2, that's the next build.
